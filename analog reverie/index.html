analog reverie

A growing archive of images, references, and visual fragments found somewhere on the internet.

what is this?

analog reverie is a visual archive of things that felt worth remembering.

Photographs, compositions, colors, rooms, people, objects, outfits, places, textures and completely unexplainable images that somehow stayed in my head.

There isn't really a theme.

If an image made me stop scrolling, it probably belongs here.

Every image links back to the original post and its creator.
